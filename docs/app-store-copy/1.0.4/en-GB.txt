[Description]
Score Chinese Official mahjong (Guobiao / MCR) from a photo. When someone wins, snap the hand: the app reads every tile and counts the fan under the 81-fan rules.

It also keeps the ledger for all four players, rotates dealer and winds, and at the end works out the fewest transfers to settle up.

The interface is in Simplified Chinese. Free, no ads, no sign-up.
